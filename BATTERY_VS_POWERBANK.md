### **5-Minute Battery Setup:**

```
If you already have the system working with power bank:

Time: 30 minutes
Difficulty: Medium

Steps:
1. Order 2x 18650 + holder + converter ($12)
2. Wait for delivery (2 days Amazon / 2 weeks AliExpress)
3. Charge batteries with 18650 charger
4. Insert into holder (check polarity!)
5. Connect to buck converter input
6. Adjust converter output to 5.0V
7. Disconnect power bank from ESP32
8. Connect battery output to ESP32 5V & GND
9. Turn on switch
10. System boots normally!

Done! Now 55% lighter! 🎉
```

## ❓ **FAQ**

### **Q: Is it safe?**
**A:** Yes! 18650 batteries are very safe when:
- You buy quality protected cells (Samsung, LG, Sony)
- You use a proper holder
- You check polarity before connecting
- You use buck converter to regulate voltage

### **Q: How long do they last?**
**A:** 
- Runtime: 6-8 hours per charge (2 batteries)
- Lifespan: 500+ charge cycles (2+ years)
- Can buy more batteries for instant swap!

### **Q: Easier than power bank?**
**A:** 
- Initial setup: Slightly harder (need to wire converter)
- Daily use: EASIER! (lighter, smaller, swappable)
- Worth the 30-minute setup!

### **Q: What if I mess up?**
**A:** 
- Check voltage with multimeter before connecting
- Buck converter protects against overvoltage
- ESP32-CAM has reverse polarity protection
- Worst case: Replace $8 ESP32-CAM
- Follow guide = no problems! ✅

### **Q: Can I still use power bank?**
**A:** 
- Yes! Keep both options
- Power bank: For testing at home
- Batteries: For outdoor/wearable use
- Best of both worlds!
